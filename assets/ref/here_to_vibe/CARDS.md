# Base-game cards (art roster)

Names, classes, and ability text as implemented in `data/base/cards/`. Use this
when posing a portrait so the picture matches what the card *does*.

## Party Leaders (6)

| Card | Class | Ability |
|------|-------|---------|
| The Fist of Reason | Fighter | Each time you roll to CHALLENGE, +2 to your roll. |
| The Charismatic Song | Bard | Each time you roll to use a Hero card's effect, +1 to your roll. |
| The Protecting Horn | Guardian | Each time you play a Modifier card on a roll, +1 or -1 to that roll. |
| The Divine Arrow | Ranger | Each time you roll to ATTACK a Monster card, +1 to your roll. |
| The Shadow Claw | Thief | Once per turn on your turn, spend 1 AP to pull a card from another player's hand. |

## Monsters (15)

Slain monsters join your party and keep the listed skill.

| Card | On-slay skill |
|------|----------------|
| Orthus | Each time you DRAW a Magic card, you may play it immediately. |
| Dark Dragon King | Each time you roll to use a Hero's effect, +1 to your roll. |
| Rex Major | Each time you play a Hero, DRAW a card. |
| Titan Wyvern | Each time you play a Hero, +1 to your next roll this turn. |
| Crowned Serpent | Each time you roll to ATTACK a Monster, +1 to your roll. |
| Warworn Owlbear | Each time you DRAW a card outside your turn, DRAW an extra card. |
| Mega Slime | You may spend an extra action point on each of your turns. |
| Abyss Queen | Each time another player plays a Modifier on your roll, +1 to your roll. |
| Anuran Cauldron | Each time you roll, +1 to that roll. |
| Corrupted Sabretooth | Each time you SLAY a Monster, DRAW a card. |
| Bloodwing | Each time another player CHALLENGEs you, that player DISCARDs a card. |
| Arctic Aries | Each time you successfully roll to use a Hero's effect, DRAW a card. |
| Terratuga | Your Hero cards cannot be destroyed. |
| Malamammoth | Each time you play an Item, DRAW a card. |
| Dracos | Each time you roll to use a Hero's effect, you may reroll the dice once. |

## Items (2 copies each)

| Card | Ability | Blessing / curse |
|------|---------|------------------|
| Really Big Ring | +2 when rolling the equipped Hero's effect. | yours |
| Particularly Rusty Coin | If that Hero fails its roll, DRAW a card. | yours |
| Decoy Doll | If the Hero would be sacrificed or destroyed, discard this Item instead. | yours |
| Suspiciously Shiny Coin | If that Hero succeeds its roll, DISCARD a card. | curse (on an opponent) |
| Curse of the Snake's Eyes | -2 when rolling the equipped Hero's effect. | curse |
| Sealing Key | The equipped Hero cannot use its effect. | curse |

## Reactions

| Card | Ability |
|------|---------|
| +1 / -3 | Add 1 or subtract 3 from any roll. |
| +2 / -2 | Add 2 or subtract 2 from any roll. |
| +3 / -1 | Add 3 or subtract 1 from any roll. |
| +4 / -4 | Add 4 or subtract 4 from any roll. |
| Challenge | Stop a Hero / Item / Magic play. Both roll; challenger wins ties. |
